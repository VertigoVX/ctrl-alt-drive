How to play
  • Collect your passenger at the green pin and follow the blue line to the red one. The meter's running. Gold pins are VIPs: they pay double.
  • A self-driving car turns its headlights on the moment it sees you. Break its line of sight to shake it.
  • DelDrive over a Del key to reboot the whole fleet, then ram them off the road. A wrench key repairs your cab.
  • Keep the cab in mint condition for tips. In Hard and Extreme, a wrecked cab is a lost cab.
